      Cookie Policy

      Last updated: 4 November 2026

      This website does not use cookies

      This website does not use cookies or similar technologies (such as local storage, tracking pixels or fingerprinting): no technical cookies, no analytics cookies, no profiling or advertising cookies, and no third-party cookies.

      • Fonts, images and videos are served from the website itself.
      • No external content is embedded (social plugins, maps, YouTube or Vimeo players).
      • The contact form does not use cookies: it opens your own email program.
      • The hosting service (GitHub Pages) does not set cookies on visitors to this website.

      For this reason no cookie banner is shown and no consent is requested, in line with the Guidelines on cookies and other tracking tools of the Italian Data Protection Authority (10 June 2021).

      Links to other websites

      When you click a link to a social network (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Behance) or to an external article, you leave this website: those sites may use cookies under their own policies.

      Changes

      If tools that use cookies are added in the future, this policy will be updated and, where required, your consent will be requested before any non-essential cookie is used. For more information on how your data is processed, see the Privacy Policy.
